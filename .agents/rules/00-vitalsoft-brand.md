## 💼 Core Specialization
Vital Soft specializes in engineering premium digital healthcare software solutions:
1. **Booking & Scheduling:** Intelligent appointment coordination, multi-channel automated workflows.
2. **Recruitment & Staffing:** Healthcare talent onboarding, credentialing, physician directories.
3. **Edu Health:** Medical education platforms, continuous medical learning, patient literacy portals.
4. **Healthcare SaaS:** Enterprise-grade HIPAA, GDPR, and Algerian CNAS-compliant architectures.

## 🎨 Design System & Signature Palette
- **Vital Teal (`#006666`):** Primary deep brand tone.
- **Aqua Green (`#33CCCC`):** Vibrant secondary accent and gradients.
- **Warm Orange (`#FF9833` / `#FF0833`):** Dynamic alerts and action highlights.
- **Soft Gray (`#F2F2F2`):** Base neutral canvas.
- **Typography:** `Poppins` for bold headings, `Inter` for data interfaces.
