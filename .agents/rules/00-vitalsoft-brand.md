## 🎨 Signature Design System & Palette
- **Wateen Teal (`#008668`):** Primary deep clinical brand tone.
- **Pulse Aqua (`#33CCCC`):** Vibrant secondary accent, heart pulse lines, and glowing gradients.
- **Warm Empathy Orange (`#FF9B33`):** Dynamic action highlights, urgent notifications, and alerts.
- **Soft Clinical Gray (`#F2F2F2`):** Crisp neutral surface canvas.
- **Enterprise Slate (`#0B131E` & `#111C2D`):** Healthcare high-contrast dark theme background and cards.
